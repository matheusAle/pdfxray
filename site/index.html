01 · Tags & reading order

Watch the path a screen reader takes.

Tag boxes, a numbered reading path and the order listed in the inspector, from one structure tree.

1 / 3Every tag in its role colour. The tree, the page and the legend share one colour per role, and problems are flagged right in the tree.

2 / 3A backtrack turns amber. The Caption is read before its Figure, so pdfxray raises a reading-order-backtrack diagnostic.

3 / 3The order, listed and compared. Read from the structure tree, then checked against paint order to show where the two disagree.

02 · Speech

Hear the page before your readers do.

What a screen reader announces, item by item, using each reader’s substitution and skip rules.

1 / 3Pick a reader. Structure (spec), Acrobat with NVDA/JAWS, Firefox, Chrome/Edge. Everything but the spec is labelled an approximation.

2 / 3Hear it item by item. Alt replaces the Figure, the DRAFT artifact is skipped, and a footnote is flagged 6 items away from its note.

3 / 3Follow along on the page. The item being spoken is outlined on the page, in reading order.

03 · Content & glyphs

Click a glyph. Climb to its tag.

Char codeThe bytes in the content stream that pick this glyph: <41>, one byte.

Every operator of the content stream, every glyph on the page, and the chain that connects them.

1 / 3The content stream, as a tree. Every operator in order, with untagged text like the DRAFT watermark flagged in red.

2 / 3Click a single glyph. ‘A’ U+0041 in /F1 at 26 pt, boxed with its baseline and advance.

04 · Objects

Raw objects, explained in plain words.

The decoded drawing instructions come first. The raw bytes are one tab away.

1 / 3Problems surface in the list. Fonts that are not embedded and streams with the wrong length are flagged before you open them.

2 / 3Instructions, explained. Every operator gets a plain note: start tagged content: H1, MCID 0, draw “Annual Report 2025”.

3 / 3Diagnostics in plain words. Declared 1,200 B, actual 1,204 B. Acrobat silently fixes this; other readers may drop the last command.

05 · Measure & page boxes

Measure in pt, mm, in or px.

All five page boxes with where each comes from, and distances between anything on the page.

1 / 3Where every box comes from. MediaBox, CropBox, BleedBox, TrimBox and ArtBox: set on the page, inherited or defaulted.

2 / 3Measure anything. Drag for a distance, Shift-click a second item for the gap. Hold Shift to snap to 45°.

3 / 3Gap and alignment, in numbers. 29.1 pt apart, left edges aligned, in every unit at once, plus trim and bleed for print.

06 · Validation

veraPDF findings, on the exact node.

veraPDF runs as a separate process. Every failure lands on the element it is about.

1 / 3Grouped by rule. 9 failed, 3 rules, 1,284 passed, with each finding listed under its rule.

2 / 3On the exact node. Badges sit on the element, operator or font that failed.

3 / 3Why it matters, how to fix it. In plain words, with an example fix. pdfxray never changes your file.

07 · Paint order

Replay the page, one operator at a time.

Step through the content stream and watch each item land on the page.

1 / 3Step through the stream. Step 211 of 377. Arrow keys step, Shift jumps to the next visible change.

2 / 3Watch it land. The current item is outlined. Whatever is painted later is ghosted.

3 / 3Know what covers it. 82% visible, 18% under the watermark: covered later by op #355 “DRAFT”.

Built for broken files
